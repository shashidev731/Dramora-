# DRAMORA Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 23 September 2026

DRAMORA is a short-form vertical drama streaming application. This policy describes the information processed when you use the app.

## Information we process
- Account information: email address and authentication identifiers when you create an account.
- Viewing data: episode progress, completion status, and timestamps when you are signed in.
- My List: dramas you save to your account.
- Technical information: information automatically provided by Android, hosting, authentication, storage, or video-delivery services may be processed for security and operation.

## How we use information
We use information to authenticate users, synchronize My List and viewing progress, deliver requested content, maintain security, and operate and improve DRAMORA.

## Sharing
We do not sell personal information. DRAMORA may use infrastructure providers such as Supabase and licensed video/content delivery providers to operate the service. We only publish content for which DRAMORA has obtained the applicable rights.

## Retention and deletion
Account and associated application data may be retained while your account is active. You may request account/data deletion through the support contact published with the current DRAMORA release.

## Children's privacy
DRAMORA is not intended for children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction.

## Security
We use access controls and row-level security for account data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes
We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date above will be updated for material revisions.

## Contact
The production release must publish a dedicated DRAMORA support contact before public launch.
